The problem

AI is changing work faster than most people can keep up

What's happening

Businesses want AI that does real work: answering customers, confirming payments, writing reports. Very few people can build it, so the people who can are in demand.

What should be true

You shouldn't need a computer science degree to build tools that save a business hours every week. Most automation is clear thinking plus the right tools, and both can be learned.

Questions

Before you start

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Tracks 0–2 need no code at all. Later lessons introduce small pieces of JavaScript and Python step by step, with labs that check your work.

How much time does it take?

The standard pace is one 60–120 minute lesson on five days a week, about 10–11 weeks in total. You can set your own pace from your dashboard; your schedule and reminders adjust.

What do I need?

A laptop and an internet connection for the building parts. Lessons, quizzes and the community work on a phone. Every tool you need has a free plan to start.

Is the certificate accredited?

It's a professional Certificate in AI Automation from Automancy Academy, earned by passing practical projects that are reviewed by mentors and peers. It is not a university or government qualification. Employers can verify it online.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The cohort bootcamp can be paid in three instalments. Payments are in naira through Paystack or Flutterwave, or in US dollars by international card.

Can my company pay or train our team?

Yes. See For business for 2-day team workshops and team seats.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Every lesson has a free Listen button that reads it aloud. Story Mode, a paid add-on, adds character voices, animated characters and offline audio.
