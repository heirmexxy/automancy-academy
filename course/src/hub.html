Questions

Before you start

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Tracks 0–2 need no code at all. Later lessons introduce small pieces of JavaScript and Python step by step, with labs that check your work.

How much time does it take?

The standard pace is one 60–120 minute lesson on five days a week, about 13 weeks in total. You can set your own pace from your dashboard; your schedule and reminders adjust.

What do I need?

A laptop and an internet connection for the building parts. Lessons, quizzes and the community work on a phone. Every tool you need has a free plan to start.

Is the certificate accredited?

It's a professional Certificate in AI Automation from Automancy Academy, earned by passing practical projects that are reviewed by mentors and peers. It is not a university or government qualification. Employers can verify it online.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The cohort bootcamp can be paid in three instalments. Payments are in naira through Paystack or Flutterwave, or in US dollars by international card.

Can my company pay or train our team?

Yes. See For business for 2-day team workshops and team seats.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Every lesson has a free Listen button that reads it aloud. Story Mode, a paid add-on, adds character voices, animated characters and offline audio.

The storyline

One business, rescued in six seasons

Every lesson opens with a problem Tolu faces at Harvest Lane. You solve it with the guide, then solve a harder version for a business you know. By the finale, Harvest Lane runs on autopilot.

Season 1–2
Tolu: I spend four hours a day copying orders from WhatsApp into a notebook. Half of them have the wrong phone number.

Season 5–6

Sage builds agents with memory, tools and human approval. Then Tolu sells the playbook to other businesses.

1 · Story panel

A comic scene: Tolu hits a problem at Harvest Lane. It frames why this skill matters.

Plain-language explanations, one analogy, and a diagram per idea. No jargon without a definition.

3 · Watch

One short, free video picked for this exact module, with "what to look for" notes.

4 · Guided build · solved problem

The problem, the plan, every step with screenshots, the finished result, and why each choice was made.

5 · Glitch hunt

A deliberately broken workflow. Find the bug, fix it, and learn the error message by name.

6 · Missions

Bronze (repeat with changes), Silver (new problem), Gold (your own business). Model answers at the back.

7 · Checkpoint quiz

Five questions. Score 4 of 5 to unlock the next module.

8 · Proof-of-work log

A screenshot, the time it saves, and one lesson. Post it to your learning journal: it becomes your portfolio.

9 · Go deeper

Free courses and videos covering the same topic, for learners who want more.

    CRM Implementation specialisation COMING LATER

    For graduates who want Path 3. Lessons 5.8 and 5.9 teach you to set up and automate a CRM on the free plans. The specialisation goes further: paid-plan features, migrations, an agent that carries out implementations, and running a full implementation for a client.

    Outline (≈ 18 hours)
    1. Discovery and requirements: map the sales process, audit the data, agree what success looks like.
    2. HubSpot in depth: paid-plan workflows, sequences, quotes, custom reports, permissions.
    3. Zoho CRM in depth: Blueprint, workflow rules, Zoho Flow, and a first look at Deluge scripts.
    4. Migration: from spreadsheets or another CRM: field mapping, de-duplication, test loads, cut-over day.
    5. Integrations: website forms, WhatsApp, Paystack, accounting tools, via APIs and webhooks.
    6. Adoption and handover: training, playbooks, dashboards, and a 30-day check-in.
    7. Salesforce on-ramp: a guided Trailhead path, and how to prepare for the Platform Administrator exam if you choose to.
    8. Build a CRM Implementation Agent: an agent team that audits a client's CRM and data, drafts the design (pipeline, fields, automations), plans the migration, and carries out each approved step through the API, with before-and-after checks and a rollback plan.
    9. Specialisation project: use your agent to implement or optimise a CRM for a real business, with a person approving every change, reviewed by a mentor.
